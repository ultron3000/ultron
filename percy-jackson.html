Series overview

Percy Jackson and the Olympians follows Percy, a seemingly ordinary boy who learns he is the son of Poseidon. Alongside Annabeth and Grover, he faces dangerous quests, ancient rivalries and prophecies that could decide the fate of Olympus. Season 1 adapts The Lightning Thief, Season 2 adapts The Sea of Monsters, and Season 3 brings The Titan’s Curse to the screen.
