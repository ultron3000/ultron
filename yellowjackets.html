Series overview

Yellowjackets follows a high-school girls’ soccer team whose plane crashes in the remote northern wilderness. The story moves between the teenagers’ fight to survive and the adult survivors’ lives nearly 25 years later, as trauma, secrets and the consequences of their choices continue to surface.
