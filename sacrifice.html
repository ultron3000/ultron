The story

Sacrifice follows Daniella Hernandez, a formidable Hollywood entertainment attorney whose clients move through a world of wealth, influence and danger. While negotiating the demands of celebrity clientele and the shifting loyalties around her, Daniella pursues answers about the accident that took her parents' lives. The drama blends legal manoeuvring, family secrets and the cost of ambition.

Season 1 premiered in 2021; Season 2 continues Daniella's story in 2026.
