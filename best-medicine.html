Series overview

Best Medicine is a comedy-drama based on the British series Doc Martin. It follows Dr. Martin Best, a gifted surgeon who trades Boston for a general-practice role in the coastal community of Port Wenn, Maine. His blunt manner makes him few friends, while a hidden blood phobia complicates the work he is exceptionally good at.
